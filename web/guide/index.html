How to listen to Persian radio online

Raadio is one free live station: everyone hears the same Persian or world song at the same moment, and the listeners decide what stays on air. Here is everything you can do.

Listen live now

  1. Play

    Tap the big button. It's live, like a radio.

    • One tap starts the live stream; tap again to stop.
    • Everyone hears the same song at the same moment, like a real radio.
    • The display says On air while you're connected, and Tuning while it catches up.

  2. Dislike

    Thumbs-down. Enough dislikes and it's skipped for everyone.

    • Thumbs-down is your dislike: it counts toward skipping the song for everyone.
    • When enough listeners dislike it, the song ends early and the next one starts.
    • Disliked songs move down a tier and come round less often; disliked a lot, they're blocked.

  3. Keep it on air

    Thumbs-up cancels a skip vote.

    • Thumbs-up takes back a dislike.
    • It's how listeners shape the station together.

  4. HQ or Saver

    HQ for full sound, Saver for less data. On a weak connection it switches to Saver by itself, and back once it's steady.

    • HQ plays the full-quality sound.
    • Saver uses much less data, good on mobile data.
    • On a weak connection it drops to Saver by itself, and goes back to HQ once it's steady.

  5. Keep it for later

    Hold play for 3 seconds to save the stream on your phone (last 3 hours). If the connection drops, it plays from there. Hold again to stop.

    • While it plays, hold Play for 3 seconds: the display shows Saving.
    • The last 3 hours stay on your phone.
    • If the connection drops, the display shows Tape and it plays on from what it saved, then goes back to live by itself. Hold again to stop.

  6. Catch a flamingo

    Tap one that lands and give it a note for the sky.

    • Now and then a flamingo flies by and lands on the radio.
    • Tap it and write a short note: it carries it off and writes it in the sky for everyone listening.
    • Notes are checked before they fly.

  7. Studio

    The door on top is for hosts.

    • Hosts sign in through the door on top of the radio.
    • In the studio they record, upload, plan shows and go live with a chat.
    • Curators also see the station's numbers.
