            Go live

            Choose the phone or computer's microphone, or the one on your headphones. Bluetooth headphone microphones usually sound thinner; wired ones and the device's are clearer.

            The music fades out and everyone hears your microphone. A chat opens for all listeners and is deleted when you end. The radio on this device goes quiet while you're live, so your mic doesn't pick it up. Other hosts can join with their studio login. You can play songs during the show: microphones go off air while one plays, unless you press Talk.

                    How to listen

                    One live station. Everyone hears the same song; listeners decide what stays.

                    1. Play

                      Tap the big button. It's live, like a radio.

                    2. Dislike

                      Thumbs-down. Enough dislikes and it's skipped for everyone.

                    3. Keep it on air

                      Thumbs-up cancels a dislike.

                    4. HQ or Saver

                      HQ for full sound, Saver for less data. On a weak connection it switches to Saver by itself, and back once it's steady.

                    5. Keep it for later

                      Hold play for 3 seconds to save the stream on your phone (last 3 hours). If the connection drops, it plays from there. Hold again to stop.

                    6. Catch a flamingo

                      Tap one that lands and give it a note for the sky.

                    7. Studio

                      The door on top is for hosts.
